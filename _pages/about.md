**Junteng Liu** is a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://zelianglab.github.io/). He is supervised by [Professor Junxian He](https://ikekonglp.github.io/). Liu earned his B.Eng. in Computer Science (or a related field) from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. During undergraduate studies, Professor Junxian He also advised him. Liu is also mentored by [Zifei Shan](https://wisenut.github.io/) (Tencent WXG) and [Prof. Yu Cheng](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~ycheng/) (Shanghai AI Lab), and now is supervised by Junxian He. He has published several papers at top-tier AI conferences, including the first author paper at EMNLP 2024.

He is currently a research intern at MINIMAX (February 2025 - Present).

**Research Focuses/Interests**:
* Natural language processing
* Machine learning
* LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning
* Hallucination in vision-language models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and interpretability
